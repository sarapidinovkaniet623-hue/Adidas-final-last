The History of Adidas

Adidas was founded in Germany in 1949 by Adolf Dassler, who was known by the nickname Adi. He started producing high-quality sports shoes, and the company's name was created from his first and last names. Over time, Adidas became one of the world's leading brands for sportswear and footwear.
